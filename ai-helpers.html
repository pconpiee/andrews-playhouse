AI Helpers

Battle-tested prompts for the hardest parts.

Each card below is a structured prompt you can copy and paste into Claude (recommended), ChatGPT, or Gemini. They're designed for the specific moments most applicants get stuck.

How to use these

  1. Open claude.ai (free tier works). Sign in.
  2. Copy a prompt from the cards below using the Copy button.
  3. Replace the bracketed placeholders like [PASTE HERE] with your own content.
  4. Iterate. Ask follow-up questions ("make it shorter," "more concrete," "now critique it"). Don't ship the first draft.
  5. Always read aloud before submitting anything AI helped you write. If it doesn't sound like you, it isn't ready.

The prompts

Honesty matters

AI is a brilliant drafting partner and a terrible ghostwriter. Admissions officers can usually tell when an entire essay was written by a chatbot. Use these prompts to think with — not to submit anyone else's words as your own. Your voice is the thing they're trying to read.
