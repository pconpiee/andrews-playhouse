Snapshot

  • Founded: 1911.
  • Undergraduate enrollment: ~11,000.
  • Faith affiliation: None — but the region is part of the American "Bible Belt" with deep Christian roots.
  • Nursing program: Traditional 4-year BSN through the College of Nursing, CCNE-accredited.
  • Sticker cost: ~$26,000/year all-in — among the lowest of any priority school.

Why this is on the priority list

ETSU is one of the most affordable public BSN programs on this list and has documented scholarships for international nursing students who meet the 3.0 GPA / F-1 or J-1 visa threshold. The university's location in southern Appalachia gives access to a strong regional healthcare system at low cost. ETSU Nursing

Nursing program

  • Traditional 4-year BSN through the Bishop Family Department of Nursing.
  • CCNE-accredited.
  • Clinical sites with Ballad Health (the dominant regional health system) and the Johnson City Medical Center.
  • Public health and rural-health emphasis runs through the curriculum.

Cost reality

Sticker is ~$26,000/year all-in. With international student scholarships and merit aid, realistic out-of-pocket can come down to $18,000–$22,000/year. Cost of living in Johnson City is among the lowest of any school on this list.

International aid story

  • Specific nursing scholarships for international students who achieve a 3.0+ GPA (or international equivalent) and hold an F-1 or J-1 visa.
  • Merit-based International Student Award available at admission.
  • Combine with external scholarships (NurseJournal database, Bold.org).

City vibe + climate

Johnson City is a mid-sized college town in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains in northeast Tennessee, about 100 miles east of Knoxville. The area is known for outdoor recreation (hiking, kayaking on the Watauga River, the Appalachian Trail) and a famously friendly southern culture. Low cost of living, affordable groceries, easy traffic.

Climate vs. Addis: Mild four seasons. Summers warm and humid; winters chilly with occasional snow. Long, beautiful autumns. The closest match to Addis on this list in terms of mild weather.

Church life

Johnson City sits in the heart of the American Bible Belt. Abundant churches of every Protestant denomination, plus Catholic and Orthodox communities. Active Christian student groups on campus — Cru, InterVarsity, Baptist Collegiate Ministries, and others.

The Ethiopian community in East Tennessee is small, but the broader Ethiopian Orthodox diaspora is present in Nashville (~5 hours west) and Atlanta (~5 hours south).

Activities + work-life balance

Affordable, slower-paced lifestyle. Outdoor recreation built into the region — hiking, fishing, kayaking. Big-school amenities (Division I sports, student organizations) at a small-town price.

Post-graduation

  • NCLEX-RN eligibility on graduation.
  • Pipeline into Ballad Health (the dominant health system in northeast Tennessee and southwest Virginia).
  • Mountain States Health Alliance and Wellmont Health System also recruit ETSU graduates.
  • Standard 12-month OPT.

Ask admissions

  • What is the typical international scholarship amount for BSN students with a 3.5+ GPA?
  • Are there clinical placements with Ballad Health and how does that pipeline work for international graduates?
  • What is the NCLEX-RN first-attempt pass rate for the last three cohorts?
  • How active is the international student community on campus?
  • Are there support resources for international students preparing for state board licensure?

Application deadline: Rolling admission for international applicants; apply early for best aid consideration.
