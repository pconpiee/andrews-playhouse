Eastern University

Snapshot

  • Founded: 1925. American Baptist heritage; broadly evangelical Christian today.
  • Undergraduate enrollment: ~2,800.
  • Faith affiliation: Christ-centered; chapel is central to campus life but participation is community-driven, not strictly required.
  • Nursing program: Traditional 4-year BSN, CCNE-accredited.
  • Sticker cost: ~$39,000/year all-in.
  • Location advantage: 30 minutes from downtown Philadelphia and its major hospitals.

Why this is on the priority list

Eastern combines a Christ-centered campus culture with clinical access to one of the largest healthcare ecosystems in the US (Philadelphia has the University of Pennsylvania Health System, Jefferson Health, Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, and many more). For a student who wants a faith-affiliated undergraduate experience with urban clinical exposure, Eastern's geography is rare. Eastern Nursing

Nursing program

  • 4-year traditional BSN; you enter the nursing major directly in your application.
  • CCNE-accredited.
  • Clinical rotations across the Philadelphia hospital system, including community-health placements in the city.
  • Christian-formation curriculum integrated through chapel and required theology courses.

Cost reality

Sticker is ~$39,000/year. Merit aid for strong international applicants can bring this down by $10,000–$15,000. Realistic out-of-pocket: $24,000–$30,000/year, plus external scholarships you secure on your own.

International aid story

  • Merit scholarships for international students with strong academics — typically tiered by GPA and test scores.
  • The Lettie Pate Whitehead Foundation does not partner with Eastern (Eastern is outside the foundation's geographic footprint).
  • Eastern has a long-standing global mission posture; the international student services office is responsive to first-time applicants.

Eastern intl admissions

City vibe + climate

Eastern's main campus is in St. Davids, a leafy suburb on Philadelphia's Main Line. The campus itself is quiet and walkable. The SEPTA commuter rail goes directly from a station near campus into downtown Philadelphia.

Climate vs. Addis: Four real seasons. Winters cold (often below freezing) but less extreme than Michigan or upstate New York. Summers humid. You'll want a proper coat from late November through March.

Church life

Christ-centered campus with weekly chapel, small groups, and an active campus ministry team. The denominational mix is broadly evangelical Protestant, with welcome for students from Catholic, Orthodox, and mainline Protestant backgrounds.

Greater Philadelphia has a significant Ethiopian community, including Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo, Evangelical, and Catholic congregations within reach of the Main Line.

Activities + work-life balance

Mission-minded student body; many students engage in service-learning trips and urban-ministry partnerships in Philadelphia. The campus is small enough that you'll know your classmates well — important during the intense clinical years.

Easy access to Philadelphia's cultural life (museums, music, food, churches of every kind). New York City is 90 minutes north by train.

Post-graduation

  • NCLEX-RN eligibility on graduation from this CCNE-accredited program.
  • Philadelphia is one of the largest healthcare job markets in the US — Penn Medicine, Jefferson, CHOP all hire actively.
  • Standard 12-month OPT after graduation.

Ask admissions

  • What is the typical merit-aid award for international BSN applicants with strong academics?
  • Which Philadelphia hospitals does Eastern have formal clinical agreements with?
  • Is there an Ethiopian or East African student community on campus?
  • What is the NCLEX-RN first-attempt pass rate for the last three cohorts?
  • What percentage of international BSN graduates secure US nursing employment within the OPT year?

Application deadline: March 1 typical for fall entry; rolling admission available.
