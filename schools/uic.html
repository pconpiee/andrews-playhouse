University of Illinois Chicago

Snapshot

  • Founded: 1965 (current consolidated form); medical roots going back to 1858.
  • Undergraduate enrollment: ~22,000.
  • Faith affiliation: None.
  • Nursing program: BSN through the UIC College of Nursing — one of the most respected public nursing colleges in the US, CCNE-accredited.
  • Sticker cost: ~$50,000/year all-in for international students.

Why this is on the priority list

UIC's College of Nursing is the largest public nursing program in Illinois and one of the top public BSN programs in the country. The College of Nursing publishes nursing-specific scholarships for current international UIC students with a 3.5 cumulative GPA, and the urban hospital ecosystem in Chicago is one of the strongest in the US. UIC Nursing

Nursing program

  • 4-year BSN with strong clinical exposure across the Chicago hospital system.
  • CCNE-accredited; consistently top-ranked among public BSN programs.
  • Clinical sites at the University of Illinois Hospital (next door), Rush, Northwestern, and UChicago Medicine.
  • High research output and access to a teaching hospital is rare among public BSN programs.

Cost reality

Sticker is the highest of the priority schools at ~$50,000/year for international students. The College of Nursing scholarship for current international students starts to apply in your second year (after you've earned a 3.5 cumulative GPA), so Year 1 cost-after-aid can be steep.

Strategy: UIC's Year-1 cost-after-aid is genuinely high for international applicants without family contribution. If you cannot cover Year 1 with merit aid + scholarships + family contribution — without resorting to high-interest international student loans — UIC is not the right school for you. A John Brown or Slippery Rock with substantial aid is a better risk-adjusted choice. Reconsider UIC once you've established a US GPA at a more affordable school and can transfer or pursue an MSN.

International aid story

  • Year 1: limited need-based aid for non-citizens; some merit awards available at admission.
  • Year 2+: College of Nursing scholarships for current intl UIC BSN students with 3.5+ cumulative GPA. UIC Nursing aid
  • On-campus employment widely available — F-1 students can work 20 hours/week during the academic year.
  • Combine with external scholarships (Bold.org, NurseJournal databases).

City vibe + climate

UIC sits just west of downtown Chicago. The campus is urban — concrete plazas, modernist buildings, and an active L train line running through it. You'll have access to Chicago's diversity, food, music, and museums, plus a world-class healthcare ecosystem within walking distance.

Climate vs. Addis: Long, cold winters with significant snow (often below -10°C in January). Mild summers. The cold is real — many international students from warm climates take a full year to adjust.

Church life

No religious affiliation, but Chicago has churches of every denomination. There's a substantial Ethiopian community in Chicago, with at least three Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo churches in the metro area. Ethiopian restaurants and cultural events are easy to find. The local Eritrean and Ethiopian student population at UIC is small but present.

Active campus ministries (Cru, InterVarsity, Catholic Center, Newman Center).

Activities + work-life balance

Big urban research university — fast-paced, lots going on, many cultural and professional opportunities. The clinical schedule in BSN years is demanding, especially with hospital rotations in a major teaching hospital next door.

Cost of living in Chicago is moderate compared to coastal cities; on-campus housing is reasonably priced.

Post-graduation

  • NCLEX-RN eligibility on graduation.
  • Pipeline into UI Health, Rush University Medical Center, Northwestern Memorial, and University of Chicago Medicine — all top-tier US teaching hospitals.
  • Standard 12-month OPT.
  • Illinois nursing salaries are above the national average.

Ask admissions

  • What is the typical merit-aid award at admission for international BSN applicants?
  • What is the College of Nursing scholarship amount, and when can current students apply?
  • How does on-campus employment work for first-year F-1 students?
  • What is the NCLEX-RN first-attempt pass rate for the last three cohorts?
  • What clinical sites do BSN students rotate through, and which hospitals hire UIC graduates most frequently?

Application deadline: January 15 typical for fall entry.
