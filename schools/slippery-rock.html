Snapshot

  • Founded: 1889. Part of the Pennsylvania State System of Higher Education.
  • Undergraduate enrollment: ~7,700.
  • Faith affiliation: None — but a large Christian student body and active campus ministries.
  • Nursing program: Traditional 4-year BSN, CCNE-accredited.
  • Sticker cost: ~$28,000/year all-in — among the lowest of any priority school.
  • International student aid: Automatic for all new international undergraduates, regardless of need.

Why this is on the priority list

Slippery Rock is one of very few US public universities that automatically awards an institutional scholarship to every new international undergraduate, regardless of demonstrated need, and increases the award for students who maintain a 3.0 GPA. Combined with a public-university sticker price, this is a structurally low-cost path to a US BSN. SRU intl admissions

Nursing program

  • Traditional 4-year BSN.
  • CCNE-accredited.
  • Clinical rotations in regional hospitals in western Pennsylvania.
  • Pittsburgh-area UPMC hospital system is within driving distance for advanced clinical placements.

Cost reality

Sticker is ~$28,000/year all-in. With the automatic international scholarship, realistic out-of-pocket is typically $20,000–$24,000/year. With strong academics (3.5+ GPA), the award scales up further.

Cost of living in Slippery Rock is significantly lower than in Pittsburgh or any major US city.

International aid story

  • Automatic award for all admitted new international undergraduates — no need analysis, no separate application.
  • Renewable based on GPA maintenance (3.0 minimum, scaling with higher GPA).
  • Additional merit scholarships available for strong test scores and academics.
  • F-1 students are eligible for on-campus employment, which can offset costs.

City vibe + climate

Slippery Rock is a small public-university town in western Pennsylvania, about an hour north of Pittsburgh. The town is built around the university and is safe, walkable, and inexpensive. Most students live on campus or in nearby off-campus housing within walking distance.

Climate vs. Addis: Cold, snowy winters (often below -5°C in January). Moderate summers. Beautiful autumns. You will need real winter clothing.

Church life

SRU has no religious affiliation but a large Christian student body and active campus ministries — Cru, InterVarsity, RUF (Reformed University Fellowship), and others. The town and surrounding region (Western PA) is part of a historically Christian belt with many denominations represented within driving distance.

Pittsburgh (1 hour) has Ethiopian Orthodox, Catholic, and Evangelical churches.

Activities + work-life balance

Big-state-school feel at a small price. Plenty of student activities, intramural sports, and an active outdoor-recreation scene (Moraine State Park is 10 minutes away). The campus is large enough to have everything but small enough that you'll see familiar faces every day.

Post-graduation

  • NCLEX-RN eligibility on graduation.
  • Strong placement into Pittsburgh-area hospitals (UPMC is one of the largest hospital systems in the US).
  • Standard 12-month OPT.
  • Pennsylvania nursing salaries are mid-range nationally; cost of living is favorable.

Ask admissions

  • What is the automatic international scholarship amount for the BSN program, and how does it scale with GPA?
  • Can the international scholarship stack with external merit awards (Rotary, AAUW, others)?
  • What is the NCLEX-RN first-attempt pass rate for the last three cohorts?
  • What percentage of international BSN graduates secure US nursing employment during their OPT year?
  • Are there clinical placements with UPMC or other Pittsburgh-area hospitals?

Application deadline: Rolling admission. Apply early — aid is awarded as applications come in.
