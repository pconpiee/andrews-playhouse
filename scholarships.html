The scholarships that actually work for you.

Most US nursing scholarships are restricted to citizens or permanent residents. The list below is filtered to what's reachable from outside the US — plus the databases and loan options of last resort.

How scholarships actually work when you're not a US citizen

  1. Federal aid is closed. FAFSA, Pell Grants, the HRSA Nurse Corps program — all US-citizen-only. Don't waste application energy there.
  2. Institutional aid is the biggest lever. Most international students who pay less than sticker price get it through merit and need-based awards from the university itself. That's why the school list matters more than the scholarship list.
  3. Private & foundation scholarships fill gaps. A handful of foundations (Lettie Pate Whitehead, Rotary, AAUW, MMEG) fund international or women-in-developing-country students. Most are graduate-level — bookmark for after BSN.
  4. Loans are last resort. MPOWER and Prodigy lend to international students without a cosigner, but loans should close a gap, not be the plan.
