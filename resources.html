About international student loans

No-cosigner loans for international students exist (MPOWER Financing, Prodigy Finance), but they typically carry 12–15% interest plus origination fees and are restricted to students within 2 years of graduation — not freshmen. If the only way to attend a school is by borrowing at those rates, the four years of compound interest will swallow the first decade of a nursing career. The right answer is almost always to pick a school further down the list where the cost-after-aid is genuinely within reach. We do not link to or recommend loan products here.
